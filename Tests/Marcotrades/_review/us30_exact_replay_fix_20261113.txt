The user reported an entry inside BL-IND and no exit at the later BR-CHoCH.

1. ENTRY: the chart showed future IND qualification over older candles.
The overlapping zone is Swing POI_BULL_1794497100, bounds 52392.5..52414.5.
At 20:03 it had zero counted touches and was NOT a qualified inducement.
At 20:06 it had one touch. At 20:10 it reached the required two touches.
Its final five-touch label was drawn from its original 15:25 source candle,
which made it appear to be an IND at the 20:03 entry.
The actual entry-price filter reports no active matching IND at 20:03 and
reports this overlapping IND at 20:10. The entry filter was not bypassed.

Change: store the first known qualification time separately from the source
pivot time. Draw the IND rectangle from that qualification time, with a
hover tooltip showing origin and qualification times. In this example the
IND shading begins at 20:10. The entry remains allowed under the existing
minimum-touch rule; future IND qualification must not cancel past entries.

2. EXIT: the LB entry veto was also deleting information needed for exits.
At 20:32 a BR-CHoCH formed at 52395.5, confirmed at 20:33. Its source pivot
was the 20:21 high of 52443.5. The corresponding BR-LB-SSL candidate covered
52427.5..52443.5. Registration rejected it because it overlapped an active
BR-IND (Demand / Supply_BEAR_1794513360, bounds 52436.5..52446.5).
That correctly blocked a new entry setup, but also left the existing buy's
protection logic without the causal LB source. It therefore missed the exit.

Change: retain such sources separately for opposing-structure exits. They
remain excluded from new entry setups, entry-rejection history decisions,
and same-direction TP extensions. Both M1 and setup-TF histories receive
these sources in the live and simulation engines. Source records are
removed when their causal structure is no longer classified as CHoCH.
