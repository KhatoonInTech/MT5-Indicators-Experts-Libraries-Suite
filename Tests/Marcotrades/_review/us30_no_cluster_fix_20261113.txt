US30 M10 no-clustering entry fix - 2026-11-13

Evidence: Logs/20261113.log, indicator run at 16:23:32-16:23:59.
Simulated session: 2026.11.12. All trading times are MT5 server/candle times.

What actually happened
20:03: BUY entry 52412.8 was inside the qualified M1 BL-IND at
52380.5..52421.5. The IND check correctly skipped it and began waiting.
20:21: The waiting filter recorded a bullish CHoCH from the 20:20 candle.
20:33: A bearish CHoCH from the 20:32 candle became known. The older 20:20
structure was also reclassified. The waiting filter retained its stale
bullish permission and did not compare it against the newer bearish CHoCH.
20:34: Price breached the original BL-IND low, mitigating that zone.
20:36: The engine accepted the 20:34 pivot at 52377.9 and opened the buy at
52381.8. Its active-IND count was zero; the price was back inside the old,
mitigated rectangle, not a still-active IND. The waiting/structure rules
should nevertheless have prevented this entry.

Root cause 1: stale IND waiting permission
Ready() previously accepted ANY same-direction CHoCH since the wait began.
It did not revoke permission after a newer opposing CHoCH, and Observe()
ignored changes from CHoCH to sweep/BOS classifications.

Fix: retain timestamped event revisions, feed revisions of the last three
market events into the M1 filter, and require the latest valid CHoCH to be
in the intended trade direction. It must be known after waiting starts and
before the selected pivot. Recheck at execution time, including cached
pivot confirmations, so later opposing structure cannot be bypassed.
Trades which never entered IND waiting retain their existing behavior.

Root cause 2: protective LB source excluded from the entry guard
The previous exit fix kept the 20:21 bearish source pivot/LB associated
with the 20:32 BR-CHoCH, but explicitly excluded that retained source from
OpposingChoch(). This was an omission in that fix.

Fix: an LB vetoed as a new entry setup still participates in the opposing
structure rejection check. It remains unavailable as a tradable setup or
a source for same-direction TP extensions. Its flag is now named
entryVetoed instead of exitOnly to reflect that distinction.
