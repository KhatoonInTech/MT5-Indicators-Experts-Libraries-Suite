Lower-timeframe LB preference - 20 November 2026

Input: InpPreferLowerTFLB = true in the live EA, backtest EA and trade simulator.
False bypasses the new preference. Lower TF supplies the actual setup, with its
normal entry confirmation, SL, sizing, TP, entry prevention and exit rules.

Same symbol/direction, strictly lower enabled setup TF:
BUY: HTF low < LTF high < HTF high.
SELL: HTF low < LTF low < HTF high.
Contained zones qualify, and the distal edge may extend below the HTF low for
buys or above the HTF high for sells. Mere boundary contact, shallower overlaps,
opposite sides and same TFs do not qualify. Any LB type can be preferred after
passing its existing entry qualification.

With M1 entry selected, the LTF setup requires its own M1 touch and confirmed
pivot within its boundaries. A pivot only in the outer HTF zone cannot trigger
that HTF setup while the valid preference exists.

Candidates exclude unqualified/future, previous-day, consumed, reclassified,
mitigated and cluster-blocked LBs. Current LB boundaries are used rather than
historical cluster envelopes. All TF cluster members register before preference
publication, preserving rejection-only cluster renewal. Waiting consumes no
setup or cluster. Actual entries retain the existing durable cluster lock.
When no eligible deeper LTF remains, the HTF resumes its existing rules;
retired setups are not revived. Multiple nesting levels defer larger TFs.

Scope: setup TFs loaded in the same program. Other charts do not supply
candidate snapshots. Existing cross-chart durable trade reservations remain.
No trade journal format changes. The simulator cache is version 31 and the
bool participates in its key. Diagnostics use [LTF LB PREFERENCE], once per
changed preference instead of on every tick.

premature cluster retirement and CHoCH-only blocking. No full MT5 recorded
history/tick replay or broker execution was performed. No speed claim.
