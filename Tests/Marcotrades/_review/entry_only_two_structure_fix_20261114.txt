ENTRY-ONLY TWO-STRUCTURE LB GATE - 2026-11-14

Behavior
- A BL-LB entry setup, whether BSL, IND, or the combined type, remains unavailable
  until a valid mitigated BL-IND is followed by two consecutive bullish market
  structure events (BL-BOS or BL-CHoCH) on the setup timeframe.
- A BR-LB entry setup follows the mirror rule using a valid mitigated BR-IND and
  two consecutive bearish events (BR-BOS or BR-CHoCH).
- An opposite BOS/CHoCH resets the consecutive count. Sweep labels are ignored;
  they are not counted as bullish/bearish structure events. If a previously
  counted event is renamed as a sweep, it no longer qualifies.
- The newest same-side IND mitigation starts the sequence used by an unarmed setup.
- Eligibility is calculated only from closed setup-timeframe bars. An eligible LB
  becomes available after the second event candle closes.

Scope separation
- Raw LB creation and chart-zone logic are unchanged.
- Entry-prevention/rejection histories continue to receive raw LBs immediately.
- Open-position partial/full exits, structural exits, opposing TP discovery and
  new-LB TP extension continue to use the old raw-LB behavior.
- Only new entry scanning, M1 touch arming and setup pre-confirmation auditing use
  the delayed entryTriggerTime.
- Live bootstrap reconstructs the same entryTriggerTime when the EA is restarted.
