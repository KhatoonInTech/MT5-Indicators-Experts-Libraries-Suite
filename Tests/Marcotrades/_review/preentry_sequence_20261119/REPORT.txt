Pre-entry opposing-structure continuation extension

Enabled only through the existing InpApplyEntryLBPolicyToPreventionExits=true path.
Existing post-entry table verified and preserved:
TP -> TP: 25% then 25% of remainder; TP -> SL: 25% then all remaining.
SL -> SL: 50% then all remaining; SL -> TP: 50% then keep remainder.

Extension: the latest linked opposing structure known when the trade opens may
serve as the first event. Its adjacent opposing structure on the SAME timeframe,
confirmed after entry, closes all remaining volume when execution price is in
loss (buy Bid < entry, sell Ask > entry). No pre-entry partial is fabricated.
Both M1 and setup TF are checked. A full continuation takes priority over a new
ordinary partial on the other TF within the same processing cycle. Existing
already-started post-entry sequences retain their timeframe and behavior.
IND and sweep-based sources use the same existing raw causal LB history.
Direction changes, unlinked sources, already-seen events, future-only links,
and opposite reclassifications cannot qualify this extension.
At profit or exact entry price, the added full-close rule does not trigger.
